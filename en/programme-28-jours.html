The programme

28 days to get going again, step by step.

No need to restart with endless or overly hard workouts. The programme gets you moving again gradually and helps you rebuild a training routine.

  1. Days 1 to 7Restart

    Rediscover the movements and regain confidence, without chasing intensity.

  2. Days 8 to 14Progress

    Gradually increase the volume and gain better control of the exercises.

  3. Days 15 to 21Strengthen

    Consolidate the basics and steadily improve your fitness.

  4. Days 22 to 28Consolidate

    Finish the 28 days with more ease and a routine in place.
