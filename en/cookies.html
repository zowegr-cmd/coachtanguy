Cookies & local storage

Cookie policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. No tracking cookies

coachtanguy.com uses no analytics, advertising or social-media cookies, and no third-party trackers. No consent banner is therefore required: in line with the recommendations of the Belgian Data Protection Authority, only storage strictly necessary for the operation of the site is used, which is exempt from consent.

2. Functional local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage (localStorage / sessionStorage / Cache Storage) solely for the following technical purposes:

  • "Calculator" app cache (Cache Storage, if you install the calculator as an app): keeps the files needed to work offline. Duration: until you uninstall the app or clear the cache.
  • SITE_EDITS2_fr / _nl / _en, SITE_PREVIEW_fr / _nl / _en and CALC_I18N_fr / _nl / _en (localStorage): content drafts, used only in preview mode by the site administrator. They are neither read nor created during a normal visit. Duration: until manually deleted.
  • dash_ok and dash_pw (sessionStorage): keep the administrator's session on the editing dashboard. Duration: closing the tab.
  • ct_prog_vente (sessionStorage): remembers during your visit that the 28-Day Restart Programme is on sale, so the buy button becomes active without asking the site again. Duration: closing the tab.
  • ct_buy_lang (localStorage): remembers the language of the page a purchase starts from, to show the thank-you page in the same language. Duration: until the thank-you page is shown, otherwise until manually deleted.
  • cookie_consent (localStorage): would remember your choice if a consent banner were ever activated. Not currently used. Maximum duration: 6 months.

This storage stays in your browser: it sends no information to third parties and cannot be used to track you.

3. External services

  • Fonts: hosted locally on the site, no request is made to Google Fonts.
  • Stripe payments: the payment buttons are plain links to Stripe's secure pages; Stripe's cookies only apply on Stripe's site (see their privacy policy).
  • WhatsApp: the WhatsApp buttons are links; no Meta script is loaded on this site.

4. How to delete this data?

You can clear your browser's local storage and cache at any time: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data (or equivalent depending on your browser). For the installed app, simply uninstall it.

5. If trackers were ever added

If non-essential cookies (statistics, marketing) were ever used, they would only be set after your prior, free and informed consent, collected through a banner where refusing is as easy as accepting, with no pre-ticked boxes, and your choice would be kept for a maximum of 6 months. This page would be updated accordingly.
